Mission briefing

You have been hired as a scientist by the International Space Agency. The agency has £10 billion to send one robotic mission to the place in our Solar System where alien life is most likely to be found.

Your job: investigate the evidence, choose the destination, design the spacecraft and pitch your mission. Work through the tabs in order. Your answers are saved in this browser, and on the Sum Up tab you can copy them all to hand in.

Remember: no life has ever been found beyond Earth. You are looking for places where life could exist.

By the end you can

  • Explain what a "potential biosignature" is
  • Find planets, moons and spacecraft in NASA’s live Solar System
  • Name the three ingredients life needs
  • Explain how extremophiles survive, and why that matters
  • Compare Mars, Europa, Titan and Enceladus using evidence
  • Plan a £10 billion mission and pitch it
