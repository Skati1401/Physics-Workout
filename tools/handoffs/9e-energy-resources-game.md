# 9e Power the town: scenarios from the past papers (school account, 22 Nov 2026)

A game for IGCSE 4.18P and 4.19P (Year 10 Unit 4), with IB A.3 (energy density of fuels) as a small IB level. Each round gives a place and a need (a picture with the facts the choice depends on: sunshine, wind, hills and rain, volcanic rock, coast and tides, how steady or sudden the demand is, space, wildlife). The student picks the resource or mix from cards, presses Check, and justifies it with advantage and disadvantage chips in the mark schemes' words. A second stage on some rounds: the energy chain (which store decreases, then turbine, generator, transferred electrically), with what is wasted by heating and sound.

Read from the IGCSE Physics Question Index on the school Drive (22 Nov 2026). **Reserved papers:** rows marked Mock or Topic test are kept for Alex's tests. Use their wording and the same kind of situation, but never the same question (its setting with its numbers). Make up the towns and numbers.

## Situations the papers use

| # | Situation | What the mark scheme rewards | Papers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Power a small group of remote homes: solar, wind or geothermal | all renewable, no pollution; solar only when sunny, large area; wind needs a windy (coastal) site, noise, birds; geothermal reliable but only in volcanic areas | May 2018 P2 TZ1 Q7 |
| 2 | Choose by location and climate: geothermal or solar | geothermal needs a volcanic or hot-rock site and is unaffected by the weather; solar needs a sunny climate, low latitude and space | May 2014 P2 TZ1 Q8 |
| 3 | Meet a sudden rise in demand, from a table of stations | wind too small and unreliable; tidal only at fixed times; nuclear too slow to start; gas fastest, coal fairly fast and large; a mixture is needed | May 2016 P2 TZ2 Q6 |
| 4 | A wind farm in a hilly area: add pumped storage | the wind farm cannot store energy; wind runs the pump when demand is low; the upper lake stores energy; released through the turbines when demand is high | May 2024 P2 7(c)(iii) (TT) |
| 5 | Hydroelectric or a wind farm | hydro responds to demand, larger output, not weather dependent; needs mountains, a large dam, flooding, permanent | Jan 2022 P2 7(a) (TT); May 2024 P2 7(c)(i), (ii) (TT) |
| 6 | Could solar supply a whole country? (area from W per m²) | area needed compared with the total; Sun not always shining; some land unsuitable or needed for other uses | Jan 2021 P2 4(c) (TT) |
| 7 | Replace a coal station with wind turbines | wind unreliable; small output, hundreds of turbines for one station; visual and noise; sites; cost | Jan 2013 P2 Q2 |
| 8 | A coastal town: tidal power | renewable, no CO₂, reliable and predictable tides; harms habitats, output varies with the tide, shipping, few sites | May 2025 P2R Q3 (Mock) |
| 9 | Low-carbon power that runs all the time: nuclear or biomass | nuclear no CO₂ and reliable, but waste, accident risk, decommissioning; biomass renewable and carbon neutral, but land use, transport, inefficient | May 2015 P2 TZ2 Q7; Jan 2023 P2 3(b) (TT); May 2026 P2R 3(c)(iii) (Mock) |
| 10 | Gas against wave power | gas meets demand and is reliable, but non-renewable and gives CO₂; waves renewable and clean, but not always available, harm wildlife, storm damage | May 2023 P2R 2(b) (TT) |
| 11 | Energy density of fuels (IB level, and IGCSE extension) | ratio of energy densities from a chart; uranium's is far higher than the others | May 2026 P2R 3(b), 3(c)(i) (Mock) |

Energy chain kinds (4.18P): which store decreases for geothermal (thermal), hydro (gravitational), a petrol generator (chemical), wind (kinetic) (Jan 2022 P2R 1(a)(i), TT); the transfers in a hydro, geothermal, fossil-fuel or nuclear station (May 2024 P2 7(b) (TT), May 2016 P1 TZ2 5(b), May 2019 P2 TZ2 1(a), May 2026 P2 4(a) (Mock)).

Words the mark schemes use: renewable (will not run out, can be replaced); reliable or not weather dependent; responds quickly to demand; no CO₂ or greenhouse gases; global warming, acid rain (with the gas); radioactive waste; habitats destroyed, valley flooded; visual and noise pollution; large area; only in certain locations.
