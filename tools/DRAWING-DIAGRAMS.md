# Diagrams students must draw (Alex, 10 Nov 2026)

Every workout and animation that covers one of these spec points includes a task where the student **draws or completes the diagram**. Each task is modelled on how the past exam questions ask for it: what the question gives, what the student adds, and how it is marked. Take the question kinds from the past papers or Alex's question indexes, never from memory. Graphs and experiments are covered elsewhere and are left out here.

Spec numbers are from the three specs: IGCSE 4PH1, IAL Physics (2018) and IB Physics (first assessment 2025).
