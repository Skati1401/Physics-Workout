Moments

TA1.30P1.31P1.32P

Moment = force × perpendicular distance from the pivot (N m). When balanced, total clockwise moment = total anticlockwise moment.

Explore — balance the seesaw

Two people sit on a seesaw. Change their weights and their distances from the pivot. The seesaw balances when the clockwise and anticlockwise moments are equal.

Beams and Supports

TA1.32P1.33P
Explore — a lorry crossing a bridge

A bridge rests on supports at A and B, 10 m apart. Move the lorry, or press Drive across, and watch the upward force from each support change.

The bridge’s own weight acts at its centre. The support nearer the lorry pushes up harder, and the two support forces always add up to the total weight.

Task 1 — walking across a plank
