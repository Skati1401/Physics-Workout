Drag the big Earth to spin it, or drag the small Earth round the Sun. Seen from above the North Pole, the Earth spins anticlockwise and orbits anticlockwise.

Why do we have day and night?
  1. The Earth spins on its axis once every 24 hours.
  2. The side of the Earth facing the Sun has daylight. It is night on the side facing away from the Sun.
  3. As the Earth spins, a place turns from the night side into the day side (sunrise), and later from the day side into the night side (sunset).
  4. The Sun is not moving round the Earth. It only seems to move across our sky because the Earth is spinning.
  5. The Earth also orbits the Sun. One orbit takes one year, and the Earth spins about 365 times in that time, so a year has 365 days. (More exactly 365¼, so every fourth year has an extra day, 29 February.)
