Why is it warmer in summer?
  1. The Earth's axis is tilted at 23.5°, and it always points the same way in space.
  2. In June the northern half of the Earth is tilted towards the Sun: it is summer in the UK.
  3. Days are longer than nights, so the ground has more hours to warm up.
  4. The Sun is higher in the sky, so its rays are more concentrated: the same energy falls on a smaller area of ground.
  5. It is not because the Earth is closer to the Sun. The Earth is closest to the Sun in early January.
  6. In December the southern half is tilted towards the Sun, so it is summer in New Zealand.
