How the model changed
  1. About 150 AD, Ptolemy: the Earth is at the centre. The Sun, the Moon, the planets and the stars all go round it. Mars goes round a small circle as that circle goes round the Earth.
  2. 1543, Copernicus: the Sun is at the centre. The planets, the Earth too, go round it in circles. Most people did not accept this at first.
  3. 1609, Kepler: the Sun is at the centre, the orbits are elliptical (oval) and moons go round planets. His model put the planets in the right places.
  4. 1610, Galileo: with a telescope he saw four moons going round Jupiter. Not everything goes round the Earth, so this was evidence against Ptolemy's model.
  5. A scientific model changes when new evidence does not fit it.
