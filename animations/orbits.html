Why does an orbit need gravity?
  1. Gravity is a force of attraction between any two masses. It is stronger for bigger masses and for objects that are closer together.
  2. A planet is always moving. On its own it would carry on in a straight line.
  3. The Sun’s gravity pulls it towards the Sun all the time, so its path keeps bending into an orbit.
  4. Without the Sun’s gravity the planets would all fly off into space.
  5. A comet’s orbit is very elliptical. It speeds up as it falls in towards the Sun and slows down as it moves away.
  6. The Earth’s gravity keeps the Moon (a natural satellite) and artificial satellites in orbit round the Earth.
