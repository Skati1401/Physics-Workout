Why do the stars look close together?
  1. A constellation is a pattern of stars as we see it from the Earth.
  2. Its stars are in nearly the same direction from the Earth, so they look close together in the sky.
  3. But they are not usually close together in space: some are hundreds of light years further away than others.
  4. Seen from the side, or from another star, the pattern would look different, or would not be there at all.
  5. A star that looks brighter is not always nearer. It may just give out more light.
