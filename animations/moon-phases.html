Why does the Moon have phases?
  1. The Moon does not give out its own light. It reflects light from the Sun.
  2. The Sun always lights up half of the Moon.
  3. From the Earth we can only see the half of the Moon that faces us.
  4. The Moon is a satellite of the Earth. As it orbits the Earth, we see different amounts of its lit half.
  5. So the Moon seems to change shape. The different shapes are called the phases of the Moon. They happen because we cannot always see all of the part that is lit by the Sun.
  6. It is not the Earth's shadow. The Earth's shadow only falls on the Moon in an eclipse.
