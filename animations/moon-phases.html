Why does the Moon have phases?
  1. The Moon does not give out its own light. It reflects light from the Sun.
  2. The Sun always lights up half of the Moon.
  3. From the Earth we can only see the half of the Moon that faces us.
  4. As the Moon orbits the Earth, we see different amounts of its lit half. These are the phases.
  5. It is not the Earth's shadow. The Earth's shadow only falls on the Moon in an eclipse.
