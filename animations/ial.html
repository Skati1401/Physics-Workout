IAL Animations

Unit 1 Mechanics and Materials

Projectile Lab

Launch up to three projectiles at once: velocity components, motion graphs, air resistance, and suvat working for the flight time, maximum height and range.

Shoot the Monkey

Fire a dart rifle from the shoulder at a monkey that lets go: aimed straight at it, at any angle or horizontally, both fall ½gt², so it still hits. Set the angle yourself to see it miss.

Free-Body Diagrams

Draw the forces on an object in 18 situations, from a book on a table to a ball hanging in an accelerating car, then check directions and relative sizes.

Newton's Laws

A puck on ice (first law), Zahra on scales in a lift (second law) and two skaters pushing apart (third law), each with two predict questions.

Inclined Plane

A block on a ramp: resolve its weight, compare static and dynamic friction, and watch it slide, stop or stay put, with a velocity–time graph.

Unit 2 Waves and Electricity

Photoelectric Effect

Light on a metal plate: threshold frequency, Emax = hf − Φ, and the stopping voltage on the current–voltage graph.

Unit 4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles

Alpha, Beta and Gamma in Fields

Fire alpha, beta and gamma through a magnetic or electric field: which way each bends, the beta fan, and r = p/BQ for alpha and beta.

Unit 5 Thermodynamics, Radiation, Oscillations and Cosmology

Specific Heat Capacity

Heat water or a metal block with an immersion heater: energy from V × I × t and ΔE = mcΔθ, with an Experiment mode that finds c from the gradient of the θ–t graph.

Thermistor Calibration and Potential Divider

Core practical 12

Calibrate an NTC thermistor in water from 0 to 80 °C, find b and R₀ from ln R against 1/T, then use it in a potential divider to find a mystery temperature (IAL CP12).

Specific Latent Heat

Core practical 13

Melt ice with a heater and a control funnel, or boil water on a balance, with a joulemeter. Experiment mode: plot ΔE against Δm and find L from the gradient (IAL CP13).

Gas Molecules and Pressure

Molecules hit the walls of a cylinder: change T, V, N and the gas and see pV = NkT, the spread of molecular kinetic energies, the mean ½m⟨c²⟩ = &frac32;kT, internal energy and absolute zero.

Boyle’s Law

Core practical 14

Trap air above oil, pump it to 250 kPa and release it 25 kPa at a time: p against V, p against 1/V and pV. Experiment mode finds N from the gradient, pV = NkT (IAL CP14).

Film Badge: Alpha, Beta or Gamma?

Predict which patches of a film badge go dark for an alpha, beta, gamma or mixed source, then expose it. IAL extras: dose and beta energy.

Random Decay and Half-Life

Nuclei decay one by one at random: watch N halve every half-life on the N–t graph. IAL extras: the ln N–t graph, the decay constant and activity.

Gamma Radiation: Distance and Absorption

Core practical 15

Count gamma rays with a GM tube: the inverse-square law (1/√C against d) and absorption by lead, ln I against x (IAL CP15), with an Experiment mode.
