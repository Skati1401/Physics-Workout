How to make it stronger
  • A current in the coil makes a magnetic field, like a bar magnet’s.
  • More current or more turns make the field stronger.
  • An iron core is magnetised by the coil’s field and makes it much stronger.
  • Soft iron loses its magnetism when the current stops; steel keeps some of it.
