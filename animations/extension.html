Extension Animations

Seeing and the Brain

Optical Illusions: Is Seeing Believing?

Six illusions to test yourself on: checker shadow, arrow lines, orange circles, striped circles, café wall and two tables. Match the lines and circles by eye and see how far out you are, then find out why your brain is fooled.

Mixing Coloured Light

Overlap red, green and blue lights on a screen and change how bright each one is to see the colour they make. Then predict the mixes and make colours to match a target.

Dispersion: White Light Through a Prism

Send white light through a glass prism and see it spread into a spectrum on a screen. Pick one wavelength at a time to find out why: the glass has a bigger refractive index for violet light than for red.

Blue Sky, Red Sunset

Drag the Sun down the sky and watch its light take a longer path through the air. Blue light is scattered out of the beam far more than red, so the sky is blue and the setting Sun turns orange. Then answer questions on the picture.

How Your Eye Focuses

Move a tree nearer to an eye and change the shape of the lens to keep its upside-down picture sharp on the retina. See why everything blurs underwater and why goggles fix it, then answer questions on the picture.
