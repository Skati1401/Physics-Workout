# Activity 13: Plot the Results (interactive-revision-lab)

Shared rules (headings, no capitals, checklist, verification) are in SKILL.md; the engine helpers, layout and equations are in the `workout-page-shell` skill.

`createPlot({host, points:[{x,y}], rowText, graph:{xMin,xMax,yMin,yMax,xTicks,yTicks,minor:[dx,dy],snap:[sx,sy],xLabel,yLabel,W,H}})` in `hookes-law-practical.html`. Graph paper (major and minor grid lines) beside the list of readings to plot (`.plot-split`), with the next reading highlighted. A click places a small cross for the next reading, snapped to the grid; clicking an unlocked cross takes it off. Keyboard: the SVG is focusable, the arrow keys move a dashed cursor one snap step at a time (announced as "Cursor at (x, y)"), and Enter or Space places the cross. Check locks crosses within half a snap step (green); a wrong one turns red for about 900ms, then comes off, with "check the scale on each axis". Use the same table the student has just completed, so plotting follows on from calculating. Follow it with a Predict on the line of best fit (one thin smooth line that follows the trend: straight through the origin while the points are in line, then curving; not dot-to-dot, not one straight line forced through every point, not a curve through points that lie on a line).
