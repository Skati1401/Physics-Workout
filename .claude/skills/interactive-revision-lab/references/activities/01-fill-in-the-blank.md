# Activity 1: Fill in the Blank (interactive-revision-lab)

Shared rules (headings, no capitals, checklist, verification) are in SKILL.md; the engine helpers, layout and equations are in the `workout-page-shell` skill.

A cloze passage (`passageSegments`: alternating `{type:'text'}` / `{type:'blank', id}`) with a word-bank of draggable chips including a few distractors that never validate correctly. Filled blanks are themselves re-draggable (to reassign or return to the pool). Good as the "Overview" tab — a single passage that name-drops every key term in the topic. **Check every gap against every chip** (distractors *and* the other gaps' answers) before shipping: if any other chip reads as a sensible answer in a gap, reword the sentence around the gap until only one fits ("increases with ___" took both depth and speed; "increases with ___ below the surface" takes only depth; "which causes ___" took force as well as pressure; "which causes gas ___" does not). Distractors should be real misconceptions (a wrong unit, "particles expand", "temperature rises while melting") that fit no gap at all, rather than near-synonyms of a right answer. Watch for pairs of distractors that fit two gaps consistently ("1 kg" and "10 N" fitted the mass and force gaps together); remove them. Bespoke code, not worth generalizing into a factory (passage rendering is fiddly).
